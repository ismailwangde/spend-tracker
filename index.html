Your bank SMS, on your home screen

Every bank payment you get an SMS for shows up on your iPhone home screen, sorted into categories. Free. No sign-up, no bank login.

Bank SMS → Your iPhone → Home screen
  • Everything stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud Drive. Nothing is sent anywhere, not even to the person who made this.
  • No account to make, no Google sign-in, no bank login.
  • OTP messages are processed on your phone and never stored.
  • All the code is public: read it on GitHub.

Before you start

  • iOS 26 or later.
  • About 3 minutes, all on your phone.

Set it up

  1. Get Scriptable

    A free app that saves your payments and draws the widget. Open it once after installing.

    Get Scriptable on the App Store

  2. Add the shortcut

    Tap the button. Safari downloads the file: tap the downloads icon in the address bar, tap the file, then Add Shortcut.

    Add “Spend Tracker”

    Set up Spend Tracker before? Delete the old “Spend Tracker” and “Spend Tracker Auto” shortcuts first, or the SMS triggers get lost.

  3. Run it once

    Open Shortcuts and tap Spend Tracker (or open it and tap ▶). Allow what it asks. You'll see Ready ✅.

  4. Add the widget

    Long-press your home screen → + → Scriptable → pick a size → Add Widget. Then long-press the widget → Edit Widget → Script → Spend Tracker.

Done. Your next bank SMS shows up on your home screen.

Using it

  • Tap the widget to open your dashboard: months, categories, budgets, rules and export.
  • Categories: tap a payment and pick one. It's remembered for that payee from then on.
  • Cash: in the dashboard, tap Add cash.
  • Same small payment every morning, like auto fares? It asks you once what they are.

Questions

Which banks work?

Debit and card SMS from most Indian banks, including HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, Union Bank, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Canara and IDFC FIRST. If a message isn't counted, the dashboard's “Skipped messages” shows it with the reason.

Does it read all my messages?

No. It runs only on messages containing “debit”, “spent” or “sent Rs”. OTP messages are dropped before anything is saved. Anything that isn't a bank payment is ignored and not saved.

Where is my data?

In Files › iCloud Drive › Scriptable › Spend Tracker, in your own iCloud. You can export everything as a CSV from the dashboard any time and open it in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.

New phone?

Your data comes along with iCloud. On the new phone, install Scriptable, add the shortcut and run it once.

How do I remove it?

Delete the Spend Tracker shortcut, delete the Spend Tracker script in Scriptable, and delete the Spend Tracker folder in Files › iCloud Drive › Scriptable.

Prefer Google Sheets?

There's also a Google Sheets version. It takes longer to set up.
