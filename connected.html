You're all set ✅

Your next bank SMS will appear in your Google Sheet in about a minute. There's nothing else you have to do.

Good to know

  • New payments appear at the top of the Transactions tab. Open it in the Google Sheets app.
  • Pick a Category once and it's remembered for that payee.
  • Paid cash? Tap Spend Tracker in Shortcuts and enter the amount.
  • If a bank message isn't counted, the Skipped tab says why.

Home-screen widget (optional)

A widget that shows this month's total, a bar for each day and your top categories.

  1. Get Scriptable

    A free app that runs small widgets.

    Get Scriptable on the App Store

  2. Copy the widget

    Copy widget code

    Then open Scriptable, tap +, paste, and tap Done.

  3. Connect it

    1. In the Google Sheets app, open your Sheet's Setup tab and copy the Connection code.
    2. In Scriptable, tap the new script to run it, paste the code and tap Connect. You'll see a preview.

  4. Put it on your home screen

    Long-press the home screen → Edit → Add Widget → Scriptable → pick Large → Add Widget. Then long-press the widget → Edit Widget → Script → pick the one you pasted.
