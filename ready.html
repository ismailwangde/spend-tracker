You're all set ✅

Your next bank SMS will be saved on this iPhone. One last step: the widget.

  1. Add the widget

    Long-press your home screen → + → Scriptable → pick Large (or any size) → Add Widget.

  2. Point it at Spend Tracker

    Long-press the new widget → Edit Widget → Script → Spend Tracker.

Good to know

  • Tap the widget to open your dashboard: months, categories, budgets, rules, cash and export.
  • Pick a category for a payment once and it's remembered for that payee.
  • The widget refreshes every 15 minutes or so. Tap it for the latest.
  • Payments not showing up? In Shortcuts, tap ⋯ on Spend Tracker and check that all 3 When I receive a message blocks have Automation turned on (under where ›).
  • Your data is in Files › iCloud Drive › Scriptable › Spend Tracker. Nothing is sent anywhere.
